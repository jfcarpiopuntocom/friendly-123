How we handle your data

We do not handle them. We do not have them.

  • Your products, sales and customers are stored on your team's devices. On no server of ours.
  • For them to appear here, they travel encrypted with a key derived from your license code — the key never leaves your devices.
  • The server they pass through only bounces bytes it cannot read, and does not store them.
  • This board writes nothing to your browser. When you close the tab, no trace remains.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no ads.

The only things we do keep, in our license panel: your code, your name and contact email, and the technical failures the app self-reports so we can fix them. Nothing about your business.
