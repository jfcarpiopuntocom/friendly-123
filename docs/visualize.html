Three pains every shop owner knows

You didn't build your business to spend evenings counting stock or second-guessing your team. These are the three that never let up.

PAIN 01

You can't be everywhere at once

You run several shelves and depend on staff who don't always know what to sell first, what to restock, or whether they're being straight with the cash and the stock.

PAIN 02

Your money hides in the inventory

Sleeping products, prices glued to cost, merchandise about to expire: working capital that gets stuck without you noticing, because you don't have time to read complex reports.

PAIN 03

Friction between control and trust

You need to delegate but you fear phantom shrinkage, suspicious voids, or cash-outs that don't add up — with no tools to audit without creating a bad mood with the team.
