The real problem

Modern software is built
to extract, not to serve.

Most of the tools small shops use today were built to sell you a subscription, not to solve your actual problems. They require a connection, they track you, they show you ads, and when the internet goes down they simply stop working.

💸

You pay every month even for what you never use

Most inventory and point of sale systems put you on a monthly plan even if you use 20% of the features. Stop paying and you lose access to your own information.

📡

They depend on a stable connection

Storefronts, market stalls and weekend booths lose signal all the time. A system that only works online is a system that leaves you stuck exactly when you need it most.

👁️

Your data is not really yours

Most software keeps your information on the vendor's cloud. If they shut down, change their pricing or have an outage, you lose years of sales and inventory history.

🎨

They force you to read cold numbers

Most systems show you tables and charts. Almost none tell you, with a single color, which product is about to put your operation at risk and which one is quietly making you money.

The color language

Five colors.
Immediate clarity.

The color system is not decoration. It is a way of thinking. In under three seconds you know the real state of your whole inventory without reading a single figure.

Red, emergency

Critical stock or expired product. Act now. This one cannot wait until Monday.

Orange, check soon

Getting close to the limit. You have a few days, but do not ignore it.

Gold, opportunity

Good margin and decent turnover. This product pays you back if you push it a little harder.

Green, healthy

All good. Keep selling. This is where most of your inventory should be.

Black, sleeping capital

Products that have not sold in a long time. Money stuck on the shelf. You decide: clear it out, promote it, or take the loss.

You need a system that tells you in plain colors what needs attention today, what is paying you back, and what is freezing your capital.

MULTIPLE SHELVES · SEVERAL LOCATIONS

The owner who cannot be everywhere at once

You have one shelf downtown, another at the market, maybe one more at a fair. Each with its own rhythm, its own staff and its own register. Selling is not the problem. Knowing which one is performing, which one owes you money and which one is under target without anyone telling you, that is the problem. You need real visibility without being physically in each place.

  • Stock transfers between locations, with approval and a full trail.
  • Side by side: sales, margin, target and effective commission per location.
  • Automatic monthly payouts with progressive commission tiers.
  • Encrypted sync across every device you use (register, stockroom, phone).
